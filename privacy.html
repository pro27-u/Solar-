Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Soulshine Renewable Energy ("Soulshine", "we", "our") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we gather, store, and utilize information provided by prospective clients, site visitors, and partners when inquiring about solar engineering, EPC contracting, or rooftop assessments across Gujarat, India.

2. Information We Collect

When you request a solar consultation or structural roof assessment via our web portal, we may collect the following contact and technical details:

• Full name and business or residence designation

• Phone or WhatsApp contact number

• Email address

• Property location (city, district, or PIN code within Gujarat)

• Solar parameters: monthly electricity bill estimates, available terrace area, and connection type (residential, commercial, or industrial).

3. Purpose of Collection

Collected details are strictly used to prepare realistic solar generation models, conduct preliminary shadow assessments, prepare DISCOM net metering projections, and contact you directly regarding requested engineering proposals.

4. Data Protection & Third Parties

Soulshine Renewable Energy does not sell, trade, or distribute your contact details to third-party marketing networks. Information is accessed solely by authorized engineering personnel and project coordinators.

5. Inquiries & Data Rights

To request review, modification, or deletion of your consultation contact details, reach our Surat engineering office directly at epc.soulshine@gmail.com.
